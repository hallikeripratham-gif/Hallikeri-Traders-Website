About Us

Sachin Subhas Hallikeri is the sole proprietor and founder of Hallikeri Traders, a leading dealer of premium quality Steel and Cement. With over 16 years of extensive industry experience, Sachin has built a reputation for excellence, reliability, and unwavering commitment to customer satisfaction.

Under his visionary leadership, Hallikeri Traders has become a trusted name in the Steel and Cement industry. The company specializes in offering authentic brands at competitive prices, ensuring that every customer receives genuine products backed by quality guarantees and exceptional service.
